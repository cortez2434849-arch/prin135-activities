Sinigang na Watermelon

A sweet and savory Filipino sour soup where juicy watermelon naturally balances the tart tamarind broth and savory pork.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly (liempo), cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 3 cups seedless watermelon, cubed
  • 1 packet (40g) tamarind soup mix (Sinigang sa Sampalok)
  • 1 bundle water spinach (kangkong)

Instructions

  1. Sauté onions and tomatoes in a large pot, then brown the pork belly.
  2. Add water and 2 cups of watermelon cubes, then simmer until the pork is tender.
  3. Stir in the tamarind soup mix and add vegetables like radish, eggplant, and green beans.
  4. Top with the remaining fresh watermelon cubes and kangkong leaves before serving hot.
